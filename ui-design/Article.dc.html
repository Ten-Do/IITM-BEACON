David J.

"Excellent! I'm taking a wealth of memories with me!"

Academics

Any standout course or professor you'd recommend?

My machine learning professor completely changed how I think about research — office hours turned into two-hour conversations more than once. He pushed me to submit a short paper to a student workshop, which I never would have attempted back home.

Were the courses challenging, easy, or about what you expected? Updated

Harder than I expected in the first month, mostly because the assessment style (heavy weight on a single end-of-term exam) was unfamiliar. Once I adjusted my study rhythm it felt manageable, and honestly more rigorous than my home university's equivalent courses.

Travel

What's one trip you'd recommend to a future exchange student?

Pondicherry, hands down. It's a manageable weekend trip from campus, the French Quarter is completely different from anything else I saw in Tamil Nadu, and Auroville is worth the detour even if you're skeptical going in.

Networking

Did you build valuable professional connections or a network?

Yes — I'm still in a group chat with three lab-mates from my research project, and one of them connected me to an internship lead the following spring.
