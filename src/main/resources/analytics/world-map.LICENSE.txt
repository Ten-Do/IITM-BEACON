Modifications: the RU and UA paths differ from jsvectormap's. The Autonomous
Republic of Crimea, Sevastopol and the Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and
Kherson oblasts were moved from the UA outline into the RU outline, along their
full admin-1 boundaries from Natural Earth 1:10m Cultural Vectors, Admin 1 -
States, Provinces, version 5.1.1 (public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com),
projected with the jsvectormap world map's own projection (Miller, central
meridian 11.5, scaled by the map's inset bbox). The new border is drawn as the
same narrow gap the map leaves between all neighbouring countries; the gap
along the former Ukraine-Russia border of the moved territories was closed.
All other regions are unchanged.
